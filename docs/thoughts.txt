-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Nominal 10Y = expected Fed path + term premium (ACM estimates the split)
Real 10Y (DFII10) = nominal - breakeven
=> REAL YIELD = EXPECTED RATES + TERM PREMIUM - BREAKEVEN
Three doors for a real-yield rise:
  A  expected rates up   -> raises gold's opportunity cost      -> BEARISH gold
  B  term premium up     -> cash pays no more; causes are       -> BULLISH gold
                            fiscal risk / inflation uncertainty
  C  breakevens down     -> deflation signature                 -> ambiguous
Gold's opportunity cost runs through door A ONLY. "Real yield rose" is ambiguous until decomposed.
Units: yield/TP/breakeven in PERCENTAGE POINTS; gold_pct/copper_pct/silver_pct are decimals.
